THE LAW
scope · enforcement · benchmark
EMPLOYMENT
1990
ADA Title I
Americans with Disabilities Act

Prohibits disability discrimination in every aspect of employment — hiring, benefits, workplace systems.

Reasonable accommodation for qualified individuals unless undue hardship; direct-threat defense; interactive process expected.

APPLIES TO
Employers with 15+ employees
ENFORCED BY
EEOC · private lawsuits
WCAG 2.1 AA
De facto benchmark via accommodation
CALIFORNIA
FEHA · 1980
5+ employees; broader disability definition; interactive process mandatory.
PUBLIC ENTITIES
1990
ADA Title II
Americans with Disabilities Act

Programs, services, and digital content of state & local government must be equally accessible.

Effective communication required; defenses: fundamental alteration or undue burden, with written agency-head finding; ADA coordinator at 50+ employees.

APPLIES TO
State & local government, incl. public universities
ENFORCED BY
DOJ · private lawsuits
WCAG 2.1 AA
2024 rule — Apr 2026 date reopened
CALIFORNIA
Gov Code §11135 · 1977
Mirrors Title II for state-funded activity — the hook for CSU ATI.
PUBLIC ACCOMMODATIONS
1990
ADA Title III
Americans with Disabilities Act

Public accommodations must give equal access to goods and services — websites and apps included.

Auxiliary aids mandated; defenses: undue burden, fundamental alteration, readily-achievable barrier removal; religious entities & private clubs exempt.

APPLIES TO
Private businesses & nonprofits, incl. private colleges
ENFORCED BY
DOJ · private lawsuits
WCAG 2.1 AA
Litigation benchmark — no web rule yet
CALIFORNIA
Unruh · Civ §51 · 1959
ADA violation = state violation; $4,000 min damages per incident.
FEDERAL FUNDING
1973
Section 504
Rehabilitation Act of 1973

No exclusion from any program or activity that receives federal funds — nearly every campus.

Reasonable modifications & auxiliary aids unless undue hardship; outcome-focused; §504 coordinator at 15+ employees; OCR grievance route.

APPLIES TO
Recipients of federal funds
ENFORCED BY
OCR (ED / HHS) · funding at risk
WCAG 2.1 AA
HHS 2024 rule — May 2026, 15+ staff
CALIFORNIA
Gov Code §11135 · 1977
Same duty for every state-funded program and campus.
ICT PROCUREMENT
1998
Section 508
Rehabilitation Act · amended 1998

Federal agencies must develop, procure, and use accessible ICT — compliance enforced through the purchase.

Undue-burden defense needs written documentation; exceptions: legacy ICT, national-security systems, micro-purchases.

APPLIES TO
Federal agencies & their ICT vendors
ENFORCED BY
Procurement holds · agency compliance review
WCAG 2.0 AA
2017 Refresh — not moved to 2.1
CALIFORNIA
Gov Code §7405 · 2003
Applies §508 to CA state agencies — basis for CSU procurement review.
WEB
sites, apps & portals

Job boards, application portals, benefits self-service. Inaccessible hiring flows draw failure-to-accommodate claims. CAActionable pre-hire, from 5 employees.

WCAG 2.1 AA (2024 rule, now reopened); carve-outs: archived content, pre-existing docs, third-party. CA§11135 parallel; ATI: scans, audits, CMM roadmaps.

No federal web rule — 2.1 AA is the litigation benchmark; apps & embedded third-party components in scope. CAADA web violation is per se Unruh: ≥$4,000 per incident.

Portals, patient pages, telehealth at 2.1 AA — carve-outs mirror Title II's. CA§11135 parallel; annual ATI reporting.

Public & intranet sites at 2.0 AA; VPATs / ACRs for COTS web products & SaaS. CA§7405 aligns state platforms & vendor ACR review.

INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS
courses, LMS, documents & media

Training content accessible on request — captions, alternative formats. CAFEHA: training access counts as equal benefits & privileges.

Syllabi, slides, PDFs, LMS at 2.1 AA. If the rule stands: password-protected course content excepted only until a student with a disability enrolls. CAATI: docs, media, Canvas.

Private ed providers: accessible exams & courses expressly required; failure triggers ADA + Unruh claims.

Timely, equally effective formats — Braille, audio, captions — with disability services; the test is effective communication. CAATI: timely access.

Purchased courseware & e-learning must conform — accessibility rides on the buy, not just the build.

ICT PROCUREMENT
purchasing, contracts & vendors

Procure or adapt assistive tech as accommodations — licenses, integrations, AT compatibility. CADuty from 5 employees.

Accessibility clauses in RFPs, VPAT / ACR scrutiny, remediation timelines in the contract. CAATI structured procurement review.

Buying accessible customer-facing tech is the cheapest defense — liability attaches to what you deploy.

Buy accessible, or document the equally effective accommodation plan before rollout.

Federal purchases must conform to §508. CA§7405 extends to state agencies; ATI requires Equivalent Facilitation memos before purchase.
