FUNCTIONAL LIMITATION KEY

Section 508 functional performance criteria, 36 CFR 1194 App. C §302. An icon on a criterion means that criterion is one the person depends on.

  • Without Vision 302.1

    Works with no sight at all, by sound or touch.

  • With Limited Vision 302.2

    Works when you can see, but not well.

  • Without Perception of Color 302.3

    Works if every color looked the same.

  • Without Hearing 302.4

    Works with the sound off.

  • With Limited Hearing 302.5

    Works when sound is faint or muddy.

  • Without Speech 302.6

    Works without ever speaking aloud.

    No WCAG criterion requires a non-voice alternative; voice-only kiosks and phone systems fall to 508’s technical chapters.

  • With Limited Manipulation 302.7

    Works with shaky, slow, or one-handed control.

  • With Limited Reach and Strength 302.8

    Works without a long reach or a hard press.

  • With Limited Language, Cognitive, and Learning Abilities 302.9

    Works when reading, memory, or focus is hard.

  • No 508 counterpart

    Photosensitivity and motion sensitivity (2.3.x) are named by no functional performance criterion.

  • Not required by Section 508. 508

    Not required by Section 508

    Section 508 cites WCAG 2.0 Levels A and AA. Criteria added in WCAG 2.1 and 2.2, and all Level AAA criteria, carry this mark.

ABOUT WCAG

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines are the W3C’s technical standard for making digital content usable by people with disabilities. Version 2.0 (2008, also ISO/IEC 40500) was extended by 2.1 (2018) and 2.2 (2023); each version adds criteria and keeps the earlier ones, except that 2.2 retires 4.1.1 Parsing. Success criteria are testable statements, grouped under the four principles, and each is assigned a conformance level.

What it applies to

Directly: web content of every kind: pages, web applications, and anything delivered through a browser, including HTML, scripts, media, and documents such as PDF or Office files published on the web.

By adoption: native mobile apps, desktop software, and electronic documents outside the web, through the W3C’s WCAG2ICT guidance and standards that cite it, including Section 508, EN 301 549, and the ADA Title II web rule.

Not covered: hardware, physical products, and closed devices such as kiosks and printers. Those are handled by the technical chapters of Section 508 and EN 301 549 rather than by WCAG.
